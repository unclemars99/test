# D000c — E000-B lower-baseline decision

## Evidence

E000-B ran on 945 PHM2010-derived cuts (C1/C4/C6, 315 each) with 48 numeric process features and strict outer leave-one-cutter-out evaluation.

Three representations were compared:

- raw engineered/statistical features;
- train-only standardization;
- train-only standardization + PCA retaining 95% variance.

### Held-out wear probe

The simple baselines are not consistently transferable across all three cutters.

- PCA performs well when C1 is held out (`R2 ~= 0.63`) and when C4 is held out (`R2 ~= 0.78`).
- PCA fails badly when C6 is held out (`R2 ~= -0.88`).
- Standardized/raw linear probes also fail on some held-out cutters.

Therefore a good average score would hide major domain dependence.

### Representation geometry

Compared with standardized full features, PCA:

- slightly increases the sharedness ratio;
- slightly improves cross-cutter trajectory-direction consistency;
- slightly lowers cutter-domain silhouette/separability.

However these geometric improvements do not translate into uniformly improved held-out-cutter state prediction, especially for C6.

The raw-feature cosine sharedness ratio is not interpreted as a meaningful cross-representation comparison because unscaled heterogeneous feature units make cosine geometry misleading. Raw remains useful as a downstream lower baseline only.

## Decision

**H001 remains OPEN.**

E000-B does NOT show that simple statistical normalization or PCA solves the shared-representation problem.

What E000-B does establish is:

1. cross-cutter domain shift is strong enough to break simple transferable models;
2. low-dimensional PCA can improve some representation-geometry diagnostics without guaranteeing true cross-domain transfer;
3. held-out-domain evaluation is essential — aggregate averages are insufficient;
4. later Process Token / self-supervised methods have a meaningful baseline to beat.

## Next

- Preserve E000-B as the feature-level lower baseline.
- Do not tune on C6 to rescue the result.
- Continue E000-A/H002c once suitable high-frequency/cycle-aligned public data are available.
- For later representation models, require improvements on **all or most LOCO folds**, not only the mean.
- Add fold-consistency as an explicit success criterion.
