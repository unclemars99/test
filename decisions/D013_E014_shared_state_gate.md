# D013 — E014 shared-state gate

## Evidence considered
E014 tested the current process-aware representation on two outcome families that are independent of waveform-derived anomaly labels: visual-quality events and downstream quality outcomes after a physical-state excursion.

## Decision
Do **not** promote the current evidence to a general shared latent-state claim.

The strongest explicit physics variables remain highly informative. The current Process representation does not show stable incremental value beyond the strong physics baseline across the two E014 tasks.

## What remains supported
- Process-aware organization of nonstationary industrial signals remains useful.
- Context-conditioned representation remains more defensible than forced domain erasure.
- Process representation may contain task-specific structural information that is not captured by a generic fixed waveform representation.
- A small position-related visual-quality subset is a plausible follow-up hypothesis, not a result.

## Stop-loss rule
Run at most one further independent high-quality truth test focused on verified physical quality (for example teardown or pull-strength evidence). If Process does not add stable information beyond strong physics features there, stop the strong `Z_shared` narrative and converge the work to a process-aware / physics-aware / context-conditioned industrial representation method.
