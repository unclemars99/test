# D000i — Ordered physical composition is not supported on PHM2010; stop tuning Process Token on this benchmark

## Experiment

E000-H tested H002e on 45 raw PHM2010 cuts using strict leave-one-cutter-out evaluation and five seeds.

Each common-phase spindle revolution was divided into three identical 96-sample tooth sectors. Both conditions used the same raw Fx/Fy/Fz samples, the same tooth segmentation, the same `Tooth -> Revolution` hierarchical encoder, the same revolution-level VICReg objective and the same frozen Ridge probe.

The only manipulated variable was whether the three tooth sectors retained a globally consistent physical order or were assigned a deterministic non-identity random order per revolution.

## Result

Mean LOCO wear R2:

- Ordered physical composition: **0.083**
- Shuffled composition: **0.105**

Worst-fold mean R2:

- Ordered: **-0.130**
- Shuffled: **-0.063**

Ordered composition wins only 1 of 3 held-out cutters and 2 of 5 seed-averaged comparisons. Its mean fold seed standard deviation is also worse: **0.557 vs 0.433**.

All five pre-registered H002e checks fail.

## Decision

**H002e = NOT_SUPPORTED on PHM2010.**

More importantly, the PHM2010 Process-Token line now contains three independent negative results:

1. E000-F v2: hard phase-normalized tokenization loses to Fixed Patch;
2. E000-G: raw waveform + explicit physical phase loses to raw waveform alone;
3. E000-H: ordered `tooth -> revolution` composition does not beat the same segmented content with physical order destroyed.

At the same time, E000-E strongly supports that the physical coordinate itself is observable. Therefore the combined conclusion is:

> **Recoverable physical structure does not automatically imply representation-learning value.**

## Stop rule

Do **not** continue tuning PHM2010 Process Token hyperparameters to rescue H002. That would risk turning falsifiable research into post-hoc optimization.

Record the benchmark-level conclusion as:

**H002-PHM2010 = NOT SUPPORTED under the tested local-coordinate, hybrid-coordinate and ordered-composition formulations.**

This does not logically reject Process Token for all industrial processes. PHM2010 milling is highly periodic and the three flutes are close to exchangeable; its wear target may depend more on signal statistics than on stage order.

## Next

Freeze the PHM2010 evidence as a falsification benchmark. The next legitimate test of the broader Process Token idea should use a process with genuinely asymmetric stages and non-exchangeable process order, such as ultrasonic welding stages, rather than inventing a fourth PHM-specific token variant.
