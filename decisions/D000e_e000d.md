# D000e — Invariance SSL removes the C6 mean collapse, but misses the pre-registered stability gate by a small margin

## Experiment

E000-D: VICReg-lite invariance-oriented generic SSL on the same PHM2010-derived 48-D feature table, with 3-fold leave-one-cutter-out evaluation and 5 seeds per fold.

## Main evidence

Aggregate comparison vs PCA95:

- mean wear MAE: 16.91 vs 22.73;
- mean wear R2: 0.585 vs 0.194;
- worst-fold mean R2: 0.416 vs -0.835;
- fold R2 range: 0.317 vs 1.614;
- cutter-domain silhouette: 0.067 vs 0.119.

Held-out fold means:

- C1: VICReg 0.606 vs PCA 0.639;
- C4: VICReg 0.732 vs PCA 0.779;
- C6: VICReg 0.416 vs PCA -0.835.

Most importantly, all five C6 VICReg seeds are positive: R2 = 0.599, 0.255, 0.139, 0.518, 0.569. This is qualitatively different from E000-C DAE, where C6 was negative on average and highly unstable.

## Pre-registered gate

The gate was fixed before inspecting E000-D results:

- PASS: all fold mean R2 > 0 and worst-fold seed std <= 0.20;
- PARTIAL: transfer improves but any fold <= 0 or worst-fold seed std > 0.20;
- FAIL: no meaningful worst-fold improvement or stronger collapse.

Observed worst-fold seed std is 0.206, slightly above the 0.20 threshold. Therefore we do **not** move the threshold after seeing the result.

## Decision

**E000-D = PARTIAL by the pre-registered rule.**

**H001 remains PARTIALLY SUPPORTED, but the evidence is materially stronger than after E000-C.**

The narrow claim now supported is:

> An invariance-oriented self-supervised objective can produce a feature-level representation whose state information transfers positively to every held-out cutter in this benchmark, including the previously catastrophic C6 domain.

This is not yet a full H001 validation because:

1. the experiment still uses engineered/statistical features rather than raw process signals;
2. PHM2010 wear labels have limitations;
3. C6 seed variability narrowly misses the pre-registered stability gate;
4. trajectory-direction consistency decreases relative to PCA, so not every representation-quality diagnostic improves;
5. H002 Process Token remains completely untested.

## Metric warning

VICReg sharedness ratios become extremely large for C6 because cross-domain cosine distance approaches zero. The ratio magnitude itself must not be treated as proof. The more reliable evidence here is positive held-out R2 across all seeds, worst-fold recovery, and reduced fold spread.

## Next

Do not keep tuning generic feature-level SSL indefinitely. E000-D has established a strong generic baseline. The next scientifically valuable step is to obtain suitable raw/process-aligned signals and test H002: whether physical Process Token structure beats this generic SSL baseline under a fair protocol.
